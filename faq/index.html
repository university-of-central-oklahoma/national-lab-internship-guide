    FAQ

    Where can I find internship opportunities?

    Browse the DOE opportunities and Non-DOE opportunities. Follow each program’s official link to confirm current openings, eligibility and application deadlines.

    How do I get started?

    Use the student roadmap to choose suitable programs, prepare your documents and plan your next steps. Start early enough to request recommendations and any required academic records.

    Can international students apply?

    Requirements vary by program and position. Check the eligibility guide and the specific announcement. Ask the program office about unclear rules and your university international office about individual authorization requirements.

    Do I need prior research experience?

    Check the program’s requirements. Relevant coursework, laboratory classes and personal or team projects can help demonstrate your preparation. Explain your own contribution and what you learned.

    Should I contact a researcher before applying?

    A brief, specific inquiry can help you explore research fit. Some programs require an established collaborator; others use a central application process. Follow the program’s instructions and the mentor outreach guide.

    What documents should I prepare?

    Common materials include a resume or CV, transcripts, research-interest responses and recommendations. The required documents and number of letters depend on the program. See documents and recommendations and templates and tracker.

    Who can help if I have a question?

    The Research Internship Advisory Committee may offer brief advice or suggest a relevant contact as members’ schedules allow. Contact the official program office for eligibility, application-system and selection questions. Students manage their own applications and deadlines.

    Does this website submit my application?

    No. Apply through the program’s official application process. The website’s checklist saves progress only in your browser. Download the application tracker if you want a separate personal record.
