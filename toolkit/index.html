    Templates & application tracker

    Use these working documents to organize the process. Customize them for each program and keep your own copy. Nothing here sends an email or submits an application.

    Download your working files

    • Application tracker · Excel — deadlines, job IDs, mentor outreach, letters, interviews and next actions.
    • DOE opportunities — Department of Energy internship and research opportunities.
    • Non-DOE opportunities — other internship and research opportunities.
    • Email and research-brief templates · TXT — editable outreach, recommendation request, follow-up and interview notes.

    Download the Excel (.xlsx) files and open them in Microsoft Excel, or import them into Google Sheets. Save your own copy before adding information. Keep sensitive identity and immigration records in the appropriate secure institutional process.

    Request a recommendation

    Subject: Recommendation request — [program], due [date, time, timezone]

Dear Professor [Name],
I am applying to [program/host] to explore [research topic]. Would you
be willing to write a strong recommendation based on my work in
[course/project/research context]?

The official deadline is [date/time/timezone], and the letter must be
submitted through [portal instructions]. I can send my resume,
academic record, draft statement and a short reminder of our work
together. I would be glad to meet if useful.

I understand if your schedule does not allow this. Thank you for
considering my request.
Best,
[Name]

    Application quality check

    Read your application as a reviewer. Can they identify the scientific question, your contribution, evidence of preparation and reason for choosing the host? Is the writing direct and accurate? Do the filenames, dates and program names match?

    Check the official portal for required letters, submitted status and confirmation. Keep an exported or saved copy where allowed.
