# FAQ

## Where can I find internship opportunities?

Browse the [DOE opportunities](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1XfAbQf3lic7tOpiT_JgilAPFzAPPrtLPZBIZlM1TGBk/edit?gid=0#gid=0) and [Non-DOE opportunities](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1XfAbQf3lic7tOpiT_JgilAPFzAPPrtLPZBIZlM1TGBk/edit?gid=1254811916#gid=1254811916). Follow each program’s official link to confirm current openings, eligibility and application deadlines.

## How do I get started?

Use the [student roadmap](students/roadmap.md) to choose suitable programs, prepare your documents and plan your next steps. Start early enough to request recommendations and any required academic records.

## Can international students apply?

Requirements vary by program and position. Check the [eligibility guide](eligibility.md) and the specific announcement. Ask the program office about unclear rules and your university international office about individual authorization requirements.

## Do I need prior research experience?

Check the program’s requirements. Relevant coursework, laboratory classes and personal or team projects can help demonstrate your preparation. Explain your own contribution and what you learned.

## Should I contact a researcher before applying?

A brief, specific inquiry can help you explore research fit. Some programs require an established collaborator; others use a central application process. Follow the program’s instructions and the [mentor outreach guide](students/outreach.md).

## What documents should I prepare?

Common materials include a resume or CV, transcripts, research-interest responses and recommendations. The required documents and number of letters depend on the program. See [documents and recommendations](students/documents.md) and [templates and tracker](toolkit.md).

## Who can help if I have a question?

The [Research Internship Advisory Committee](committee.md) may offer brief advice or suggest a relevant contact as members’ schedules allow. Contact the official program office for eligibility, application-system and selection questions. Students manage their own applications and deadlines.

## Does this website submit my application?

No. Apply through the program’s official application process. The website’s checklist saves progress only in your browser. Download the application tracker if you want a separate personal record.
