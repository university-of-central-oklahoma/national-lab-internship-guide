# Research Internship Advisory Committee

The Research Internship Advisory Committee is a faculty contact group for brief advice on national laboratory research internships, as members’ schedules allow.

## Members & contact information

Start with the student guide and opportunity spreadsheet. If you still have a question, email a relevant committee member with your major, research interest and one specific question.

## What the committee can help with

- Offer brief advice on research interests and suitable internship pathways.
- Point students toward relevant faculty or official program contacts when possible.
- Share useful opportunities as they become available.

Support depends on each member’s availability. Students manage their own applications, deadlines and follow-up. Contact the internship program office for official eligibility, application-system or selection questions.
