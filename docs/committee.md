# Research Internship Advisory Committee

The Research Internship Advisory Committee helps UCO students identify national laboratory research opportunities, prepare applications and connect with appropriate faculty and program contacts. Official program offices determine eligibility and selection.

## How the committee supports students

- Help students identify research interests and suitable internship pathways.
- Explain application materials, recommendation timelines and interview preparation.
- Coordinate faculty introductions, application clinics and research-fit conversations.
- Maintain the shared opportunity spreadsheet and route program-specific questions to the appropriate office.
- Connect students with career services, the writing center, accessibility services and the international office as needed.

The activities below are a suggested operating guide. Committee membership does not imply that each member provides every service or that office hours have been scheduled.

## A six-week launch plan

1. **Week 1 — assign ownership.** Confirm the team, office-hour location, contact method and escalation routes. Review this guide and current program sources.
2. **Week 2 — invite broadly.** Reach introductory and advanced courses, community-college transfer groups, graduate cohorts and student organizations. Describe several research and operations routes.
3. **Week 3 — run a fit clinic.** Help students identify academic level, research interests and official eligibility questions. Avoid collecting sensitive status records.
4. **Week 4 — hold a document workshop.** Review resumes, research statements and reference packets; refer writing and academic questions to specialists.
5. **Week 5 — practice outreach and interviews.** Ask alumni to share approved experiences; arrange faculty-led mock interviews.
6. **Week 6 — check completion and hand off.** Remind students to verify submissions and letters, then follow official decision timelines. Archive reusable workshop material for the next cohort.

These are recommended activities, not scheduled events. Add actual dates and contacts before publicizing them.

## Work backward from every promoted deadline

| Before deadline | Committee action |
| --- | --- |
| 8–12 weeks | Verify official announcement, eligibility, host options and application dates |
| 6 weeks | Promote opportunities and explain recommendation lead time |
| 4 weeks | Offer document/research-fit review with faculty support |
| 2 weeks | Remind students to check portal invitations and missing materials |
| 3–5 days | Share a submission checklist and official support contact |
| After submission | Offer interviews practice and refer status questions appropriately |

Always include the **official URL, term, deadline, timezone, academic level and known citizenship rule**. Re-check before sending reminders. Avoid copying an old deadline into a new term.

## Office-hours triage

For a portal problem, send students to official program support. For research fit, refer to the faculty sponsor or a relevant faculty member. For individual authorization, refer to the international office and lab’s designated staff. For housing/funding questions, use the program coordinator. Log the type of question and resolution without storing private records.

## Measure what helps

With voluntary participation, track workshop attendance, completed applications, interviews and appointments in aggregate. Ask what blocked students: unclear criteria, late references, funding uncertainty, inaccessible processes or weak research matches. Use the findings to improve support, not to compare students publicly.

Avoid collecting passport, visa, banking, transcript or recommendation files in committee working files. Students retain their own application records; faculty retain confidential letters.

## End-of-cycle handoff

Update the source-review dates and next cycle’s known calendar. Remove unverified or departed contacts. Save approved workshop slides, common questions and a brief lessons-learned note. Arrange continuity when committee membership changes.

Maintain opportunity listings in the [shared spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1XfAbQf3lic7tOpiT_JgilAPFzAPPrtLPZBIZlM1TGBk/edit?usp=sharing). The website’s application checklist is local to each browser; it is not a department application database or submission portal.
