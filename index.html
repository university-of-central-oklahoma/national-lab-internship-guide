    Choose a route that fits you

    Your starting point A useful first stop
    Undergraduate or recent graduate DOE SULI, then direct laboratory postings
    Master’s student Direct graduate internships; check the opportunities spreadsheet
    International student without permanent residency Eligibility guide, then international pathways and individual job rules
    Faculty mentor or department coordinator Faculty guide and Research Internship Advisory Committee
    Know the name: DOE’s undergraduate laboratory program is SULI — Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internships. “SURI” can refer to unrelated university programs. Check the sponsor before applying.

    Your next 30 minutes

    Pick three opportunities. Write down why each fits your interests, record the official application deadline and timezone, and identify one person who can answer a specific question. Then open the application toolkit and start your checklist.
